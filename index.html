Lokomaikaii Kai

Internal publishing tool of the Lokomaikaii outrigger canoe club, Cabo Frio, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

What it is

Lokomaikaii Kai is a private tool used only by the Lokomaikaii canoe club to prepare and publish the club's own videos and photos on the club's own social media accounts, including TikTok. Every post is approved by the club owner before it is sent. The tool is not offered to the public and does not publish on behalf of other people.

How it works with TikTok

  1. The club owner connects the club's TikTok account once, using TikTok Login Kit.
  2. A finished video is shown to the owner for approval.
  3. After approval, the tool sends the video to the club's TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

The tool does not read or collect data from viewers, followers or other TikTok users.
