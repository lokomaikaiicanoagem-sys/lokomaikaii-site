Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Lokomaikaii Kai is an internal publishing tool of the Lokomaikaii canoe club, Cabo Frio, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It is used by the club's owner to publish the club's own content on the club's own social media accounts.

2. What data the tool handles

When the club owner connects the club's TikTok account, the tool receives, through TikTok Login Kit, the basic profile information of that account (account identifier, display name and avatar) and an access token that allows it to send videos to that account. The tool also handles the videos and captions that the club itself prepares for publication.

The tool does not collect, read or store personal data from viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to identify the connected account and to publish the club's approved content on it. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for profiling.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in a protected store with restricted access and are never published. Data is kept only while the connection is needed.

5. Your rights and revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and services), which immediately removes the tool's access. Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018), data subjects may request access, correction or deletion of their personal data by contacting us.

6. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: Instagram @lokomaikaiicanoa.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.

Português

O Lokomaikaii Kai é uma ferramenta interna do clube Lokomaikaii. Ao conectar a conta do TikTok do clube, a ferramenta recebe as informações básicas do perfil (identificador, nome e foto) e um token de acesso para enviar vídeos a essa conta. Ela não coleta nem armazena dados pessoais de espectadores, seguidores ou outros usuários. Os dados são usados apenas para publicar o conteúdo aprovado do clube, não são vendidos nem compartilhados, e os tokens ficam em um local protegido. O titular da conta pode desconectar o app a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok. Direitos previstos na LGPD (acesso, correção e exclusão) podem ser solicitados pelo Instagram @lokomaikaiicanoa.
